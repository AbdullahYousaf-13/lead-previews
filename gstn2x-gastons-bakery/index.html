Meet Mathieu

The hands behind the dough

Every loaf at Gaston's starts the same way: naturally leavened, slowly fermented, and built without a single artificial preservative, dye, or sweetener. It's a slower way to bake — and it's the only way pastry chef Mathieu and the team do it.

Even the flour is homegrown, in a sense: Gaston's mills its own all-purpose, whole wheat, and bread flours from wheat grown right here in Idaho and Montana, the same flour that goes into every batard, boule, and croissant that leaves the Overland Rd bakery.

Wholesale

Baked for the Treasure Valley's restaurants, cafés & markets

Gaston's supplies restaurants, cafes, breweries, supermarkets, and hotels across the Treasure Valley. Orders placed by 11am ship the next day, seven days a week, with deliveries rolling out starting at 4am daily. Outside the Boise area, Gaston's works with US Foods, SYSCO, and Shamrock to get bread where it needs to go — and ships bread and croissants nationwide through Sur La Table.
