Through the Years

Two crafts, one family, since 1967.

Nearly six decades of the same two trades, done the same careful way — custom draperies made by hand, and furniture given a second life through full upholstery.

Custom Draperies

Nothing insulates better than custom draperies hand made by our experienced master seamstresses.

In Tucson, that matters — well-made drapery helps keep the heat out and the cold in. Browse thousands of fabric samples right in our showroom, take them home to view in your own light, and work with our designers to get the fit and look right.

  • Thousands of fabric samples to browse and take home

  • Top treatments, roman shades, and other window coverings

Furniture Upholstery

Full-service furniture upholstery and repairs for Tucson and surrounding areas.

Our master upholsterers recover furniture in new designer fabric, repair damaged springs, reinforce the frame, and replace webbing. Cushions are re-stuffed with new high-density foam wrapped in fresh Dacron. We also make custom bedspreads and pillows to your exact specifications.

  • Pick-up and delivery in Tucson, Vail, Marana, Oro Valley, Sahuarita & Green Valley
  • A-plus rating with the BBB
