How Heroes Only Started

After a career in warehousing and retail management, Eric took a gamble and enrolled in barber college while living in Sebring, FL. In 2009 he earned his barber license and opened "House of Style," a small 400 sq. ft. sports-themed shop in Avon, FL.

The sudden death of his father in 2010 brought Eric back to Brandon, FL to be closer to his mom — and a childhood love of comic books, from his years growing up in NY, turned into the idea for Heroes Only. In December 2012, Heroes Only Barber Shop opened its doors on Lithia Pinecrest and C.R. 39. Four years later, it moved to its current home on Lithia Pinecrest Road, centrally located for Valrico, FishHawk, and Lithia clients — and the shop supports local schools, clubs, and non-profits in the area.
