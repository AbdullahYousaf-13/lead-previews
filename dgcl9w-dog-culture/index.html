Our Story

Megan & Steve Mussman

Locally owned and operated by two dog lovers. Megan and Steve's love for dogs began growing up with dogs in both their family homes, and they've since extended that love to their son, who adores their three — well, four — dogs.

As Greenville residents, Megan and Steve saw a real need for doggie businesses in the downtown area and established their enrichment facility in 2018. It quickly flourished, and in 2023 they expanded to a second location in Mauldin. They also love giving back, often holding fundraisers through Dog Culture in support of local rescues.

Before You Visit

How old does my dog need to be?

A minimum of 16 weeks old, and spayed or neutered by 7 months.

What vaccines are required?

Current Rabies, Bordetella, DHPP and Canine Influenza vaccines, plus flea, tick and heartworm prevention.

Are there breed restrictions?

No — Dog Culture has no breed restrictions.

Do I need to do anything before my dog's first day?

Yes — a meet-and-greet is required before your dog can start daycare.

Can my dog come straight to a Culture Sleepover?

Sleepovers require your dog to have already established a history with daycare first.

Can I tour the facility?

Dog Culture doesn't offer facility tours, to protect the dogs in their care from disruption — but live camera feeds are available by appointment.
