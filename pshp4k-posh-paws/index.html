Meet Your Groomer

Seta Chorbadjian

Seta stumbled into grooming by chance over a decade ago — and stayed for the love and compassion she found for every pet's wellbeing. She trained under leaders in the pet grooming industry, studying breed information, health and wellness, sanitation, safety and pet nutrition.

She's a Certified Feline Master Groomer with over ten years grooming cats exclusively, and an International Certified Pet Esthetician (ICPE) through the International Association of Professional Esthetic Groomers. In May 2023 she earned certification in skin and coat issues for cats and dogs, and works directly with clients' vets on dermal symptoms. Clients travel from across Greenville and neighboring North Carolina, Georgia — even as far as Florida.
