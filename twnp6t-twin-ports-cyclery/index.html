Our Story

Fifty Years on Duluth's Trails

Twin Ports Cyclery has been a fixture on W 3rd St since 1975 — a year-round bike shop built around a genuine love for biking and a personalized approach to every fitting and every repair.

We'd rather give you honest advice than make a sale. That's meant carrying real, dependable brands, keeping the parts you need in stock, and treating every customer like a neighbor — because most of them are.

"Our full-service repair & accessories shop will put your needs above our bottom line."
