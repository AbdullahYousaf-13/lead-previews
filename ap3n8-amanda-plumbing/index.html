Our Story

Family-owned since 1995

Amanda Plumbing was founded in 1995 by Amanda Hines' father, a former Marine. Today it's a second-generation, family-owned company run by Amanda Hines and co-owner Hayden Taylor — a veteran- and women-owned operation built on straightforward service, not high-pressure sales tactics.

Fully licensed (Ohio Plumbing Contractors License #49045) and BBB A+ accredited, with 24/7 emergency availability and senior, veteran, and first responder discounts for residential and commercial customers throughout Central Ohio.
